# Governance Model

Execution-Owned Enterprises combine leadership authority with structured participation from the execution network.

The governance architecture is designed to preserve stability, accountability, and long-term strategic coherence.

---

# Governance Layers

The enterprise operates across three governance layers.

---

## Founders / Board

Founders and the board define the strategic direction of the enterprise.

Responsibilities include:

- long-term vision
- enterprise integrity
- strategic expansion
- capital strategy
- leadership appointments

Founders retain ultimate responsibility for protecting the enterprise architecture.

---

## Domain Heads

Domain Heads operate the enterprise.

They coordinate execution, manage operations, and ensure service quality across the organization.

Responsibilities include:

- operational design
- execution coordination
- performance management
- system optimization

Domain Heads are appointed by founders or the board.

---

## Execution Network

The Execution Network performs the operational work that sustains the enterprise.

Executors participate in governance through structured participation mechanisms.

Their influence grows with:

- contribution
- quality
- reputation

---

# Strategic Governance

Strategic decisions follow a dual authority structure.

Founders / Board → 51%  
Execution Network → 49%

This model ensures:

- strategic continuity
- execution network participation
- protection against internal capture
- avoidance of governance paralysis

---

# Operational Governance

Operational decisions are delegated to Domain Heads.

These include:

- workflow design
- service policies
- logistics rules
- operational improvements

This separation allows the enterprise to maintain efficiency while preserving participatory governance for strategic matters.

---

# Leadership Accountability

Domain Heads are not elected, but they remain accountable.

Leadership review mechanisms may be triggered by:

- systemic operational failure
- persistent quality breakdowns
- serious misconduct
- sustained loss of trust within the execution network

Final leadership decisions remain under founder authority.
