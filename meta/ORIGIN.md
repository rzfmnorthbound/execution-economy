# Origin of the Execution Economy

The concept of the **Execution Economy** emerged from the observation that many modern platform businesses separate value creation from value participation.

In these systems, the people performing the work — drivers, couriers, operators, technicians, and service providers — generate the real operational value of the platform.

However, ownership, decision-making power, and economic participation remain concentrated at the platform level.

This creates a structural imbalance where execution is essential but institutionally marginal.

The Execution Economy proposes a structural evolution of the enterprise model.

Instead of treating execution as a disposable input, the enterprise architecture recognizes the **execution network as a core asset** and integrates it into governance, reputation systems, and economic participation.

The framework presented in this repository introduces:

- the concept of the Execution Economy
- the Execution-Owned Enterprise model
- a governance architecture combining leadership and network participation
- an economic system based on execution units, quality multipliers, and reputation capital

This repository represents the **first public structured articulation of the framework**.

The ideas presented here are intended as a foundation for experimentation, further research, and real-world enterprise implementation.
