# Quick Start Guide

This guide outlines a high-level roadmap for building your first Execution-Owned Enterprise.

## 1. Understand the framework

Read the [Manifesto](../MANIFESTO.md) and the [Enterprise model](../model/MODEL.md) to grasp the principles, governance and economic mechanisms.

## 2. Choose a service domain

Identify a service industry where network effects and execution quality matter—delivery, logistics, ride sharing, home services, technical field services or similar.

## 3. Map the roles

Define the founders/board, domain heads (operations managers) and the execution network (people performing the work). Clarify responsibilities and accountability.

## 4. Define execution metrics

Design your Execution Units (tasks), Quality Multipliers (ratings, reliability, punctuality) and Reputation Capital (network contributions). These metrics will drive participation and compensation.

## 5. Decide revenue distribution

Allocate revenue across the execution pool, operations pool, platform infrastructure and founders/investors. Ensure that the execution pool represents a significant share.

## 6. Set up governance

Implement a voting or signalling mechanism where strategic decisions are split 51/49 between founders and the execution network. Use simple digital tools (surveys, voting apps) to start.

## 7. Pilot with a small network

Start with a small group of executors and customers. Test the metrics, feedback loops and payout structures. Gather feedback and adjust.

## 8. Iterate and scale

Refine the model based on pilot insights. Expand the network, onboard more executors, and gradually add new service areas. Continuously align incentives.

## 9. Document and share

Contribute your experiences back to the Execution Economy community. Share learnings, challenges and best practices.
